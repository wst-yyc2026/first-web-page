Facts About Calgary

Calgary is a lively prairie city known for culture, outdoor adventure, and big events.

1

Calgary is often called the “Stampede City” because of the world-famous Calgary Stampede.

2

The city sits at the foothills of the Canadian Rockies, making it a gateway to mountain adventures.

3

Calgary’s skyline is marked by the Calgary Tower, a landmark visible from many parts of the city.

4

It has a strong energy industry history, which helped shape its modern identity and economy.

5

Calgary is home to one of Canada’s most used public transit systems, the CTrain.

6

The city is known for sunny skies and a lot of bluebird days, especially in the winter.

7

Calgary hosts many festivals, including music, food, and cultural events year-round.

8

It is a great base for exploring Banff, Lake Louise, and other famous Alberta destinations.
